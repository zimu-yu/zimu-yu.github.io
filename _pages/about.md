I am **Zimu Yu**, a research assistant working at the intersection of **machine learning and computational biology**. My current research focuses on **protein variant effect prediction, mutational epistasis, protein fitness landscapes, and protein engineering**.

I am currently a Research Assistant at **Tsinghua University**, **Zhongnan University of Economics and Law**, and **Emory University**. My long-term goal is to develop accurate and efficient computational methods for reconstructing protein fitness landscapes and using them to guide directed evolution.
